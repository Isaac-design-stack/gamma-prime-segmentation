# Licence guidance

No licence has been imposed automatically on the research data or images. Before public release, confirm ownership and redistribution rights for the 110 source micrographs and choose licences with the relevant rights holder/institution.

Common choices, only when you have authority to apply them:
- source code: MIT or BSD-3-Clause;
- original research data/annotations: CC BY 4.0 or CC0;
- third-party images: retain their original terms and do not relicense them.

Add the final licence file(s) before publishing the Zenodo record.
